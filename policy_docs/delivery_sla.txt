DELIVERY SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Standard Delivery Targets:
- Metro cities (Sao Paulo, Rio de Janeiro): 3-5 business days
- State capitals: 5-8 business days  
- Other cities: 7-12 business days

Sellers are required to dispatch orders within 2 business days of order 
confirmation. Failure to dispatch within this window may result in 
seller performance penalties.

Late Delivery Compensation: If delivery exceeds the estimated delivery 
date shown at checkout by more than 5 days, customer is eligible for 
a shipping cost refund.

High-Risk Categories for Delay: Furniture and large electronics due to 
size/weight handling requirements. Customers ordering from these 
categories should expect delivery toward the higher end of the SLA window.

Carrier Performance: Sellers using carriers with delivery success rate 
below 90% over a rolling 30-day period are flagged for review.
