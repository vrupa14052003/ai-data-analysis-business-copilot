RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a return within 7 days of delivery for most product categories.

Electronics: Returns accepted within 7 days if item is unopened or defective. 
Opened electronics with no defect are not eligible for return.

Furniture: Returns accepted within 10 days due to assembly complexity. 
Customer bears return shipping cost unless item arrived damaged.

Fashion and Apparel: Returns accepted within 15 days if tags are still attached 
and item is unworn.

Beauty and Health: Returns NOT accepted on opened products due to hygiene 
regulations, except in case of manufacturing defect.

Damaged or Defective Items: Full refund or replacement offered at no cost 
to customer, regardless of category, if reported within 48 hours of delivery 
with photo evidence.

Refund Processing Time: 5-7 business days after returned item is received 
and inspected at our warehouse.
