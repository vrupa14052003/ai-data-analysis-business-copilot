SELLER PERFORMANCE AGREEMENT - KEY TERMS

Minimum Performance Standards:
- Order defect rate must remain below 2%
- Average review score must remain at or above 3.5 stars
- Late dispatch rate must remain below 5%

Categories Requiring Additional Quality Checks:
Electronics and Beauty/Health products require photo documentation of 
item condition before dispatch due to higher return sensitivity in 
these categories.

Penalty Structure:
First violation of performance standards: Warning issued, 30-day 
improvement window granted.
Second violation within 90 days: Listing visibility reduced by 50%.
Third violation: Seller account suspended pending review.

Furniture Category Specific Terms:
Due to higher logistics complexity and historically lower review scores 
in this category, Furniture sellers must maintain a dedicated customer 
service response time of under 24 hours for any delivery or 
quality-related inquiry.
